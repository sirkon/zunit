## Resolutions restated as fact

14. The duration sign grammar closes as proposed: an optional single leading sign `-` or `+` applies to the whole literal and
    precedes all components; components stay unsigned magnitudes. `-1h30m` is -(1h30m); `1h-30m`, `1h+30m` and `--5s` are
    grammar errors; `-0` and `+0` decode to zero. Canonical encoding writes `-` for negative values and no sign otherwise.
15. The duration suffix set stays exactly the six ASCII spellings `ns | us | ms | s | m | h`, case-sensitive. The non-ASCII
    microsecond spellings `µs` (U+00B5 micro sign) and `μs` (U+03BC Greek small mu) are rejected as stray bytes, and `encode`
    emits `us`. `mks` stays dead.
16. A suffixless duration component is legal only when it is the entire literal: `5` is 5 ns and `-30` is -30 ns, while `1h30`
    is a grammar error — silently reading it as 1 h + 30 ns would be a footgun where `1h30m` was meant. Zero-valued suffixed
    components stay fine (`0h0m` decodes to 0).
